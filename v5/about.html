Our Story

From a Pier in
Chattogram.

S & S Logistics was born in 2010 from a simple conviction: that South Asia's extraordinary natural wealth — its teeming waters, fertile deltas, and sun-drenched orchards — deserved a world-class conduit to global buyers.

Our founders began by aggregating fish catches from small-scale fishermen along the Chittagong coastline and connecting them to buyers in the Middle East. The trust they built — with fishermen, with freight forwarders, with customs authorities — became the bedrock of everything S & S stands for today.

Over fifteen years, we expanded into tropical fruits, vegetables, jute, fabrics, leather, and industrial scrap, always with the same principle: absolute quality at every stage of the supply chain, from source to shelf.

Today S & S Logistics serves importers across Europe, the Middle East, East Asia, and North America, with two dedicated brand verticals — AquaBounty for seafood and TropiqHarvest for tropical fruits — and more to follow.
