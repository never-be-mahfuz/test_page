TropiqHarvest by S&S Logistics

Bridging Traditional Farming
& Global Demand

Operating under the trusted umbrella of Sufian & Safina Logistics BD, TropiqHarvest is our dedicated fresh produce division. Rooted in the fertile deltas and tropical climates of South Asia, we partner directly with generational farmers.

Our commitment is two-fold: uplifting local agriculture and delivering impeccable, export-grade fruits that meet strict international phytosanitary standards. We don't just export fruits — we export a rich agricultural heritage.

Seasonal Spotlight

The King of Fruits is Here

The South Asian Mango season is a globally awaited event. Renowned for their vibrant colour, intoxicating aroma, and unparalleled sweetness — our 15 varieties are hand-picked at peak maturity for export.

Export-Grade Packaging

Our mangoes undergo hot water treatment (HWT) or irradiation as per importing country requirements. Packed in 3 kg, 4 kg, or 5 kg corrugated fiberboard boxes with breathable liners to ensure they reach you exactly as nature intended.
