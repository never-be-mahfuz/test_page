Our Story

Built on the
Wealth of the Bay

Founded in Chattogram, Sufian & Safina Logistics began as a small fish-trading operation connecting local fishermen to regional buyers. Over fifteen years, we have grown into a multi-category export house trusted by importers across four continents.

Our roots in South Asia give us unmatched access to the finest produce — from the warm waters of the Bay of Bengal to the lush orchards of the Bengal Delta — while our global partnerships ensure every shipment meets the strictest international standards.
