First Time in Trade?

We Guide
New Traders.

International trade is complex — especially for businesses entering it for the first time. Regulations, documentation, Incoterms, customs duties, and freight booking can be overwhelming without a guide.

At S & S, we don't just move your cargo — we educate you. Whether you're a startup exporter in Bangladesh or an overseas buyer importing for the first time, our team will walk you through every step.

"We treat every new trader the way we wished someone had treated us when we first started — with patience, honesty, and no hidden costs."

Book a Free Consultation 💬 WhatsApp Us
01

Free Trade Consultation

Before any commitment, we sit down (in person or over video call) to understand your business, product, and target market — completely free of charge.

02

Incoterms & Pricing Guidance

We explain FOB, CIF, EXW, and DDP in plain language and help you pick the right trade terms for your shipment and risk appetite.

03

Document Checklist & Preparation

We provide a tailored document checklist for your specific product and destination country, then help you prepare or procure every certificate required.

04

Customs Duty & Regulatory Advisory

We advise on import duties, tariff classifications, sanitary requirements, and any country-specific regulations that apply to your goods — before you ship.

05

Ongoing Support & Follow-Up

Once your first shipment is done, we debrief, review any issues, and help you build a repeatable process for future shipments — so you grow with confidence.
