About AquaBounty

Sourced with Care,
Exported with Pride

AquaBounty is the dedicated fish and marine export division of Sufian & Safina Logistics BD — headquartered in Chattogram, Bangladesh, the port city at the heart of South Asia's seafood industry.

We source from the iconic river systems of Bangladesh — the Padma, Meghna and Jamuna — and from the rich coastal waters of the Bay of Bengal. Our portfolio spans four processing categories: whole frozen fresh-water fish, premium sea-water species, ready-to-cook preparations, and traditional sun-dried products.

From the iconic Hilsa (Ilish) to export-grade Vannamei Shrimp, Pomfret, Tuna, Barracuda and over a hundred other varieties, every product leaves our facility with full cold-chain integrity and all required export documentation.

From Source to Shore

Our Export Process

01
Step 01
Sustainable Sourcing

Ethically harvested from partner fishermen and certified aquaculture farms across Bangladesh's river systems and Bay of Bengal coast.

02
Step 02
Quality & Safety Inspection

Every batch undergoes rigorous grading, freshness assessment, size sorting and microbiological testing at our HACCP-certified facility.

03
Step 03
Processing & IQF Freezing

Cleaned, trimmed and flash-frozen using IQF or block freezing at -35°C. Whole fish, cut pieces, fillets or value-added formats produced to buyer spec.

04
Step 04
Certification & Packing

Halal, HACCP, health and phytosanitary certificates issued. Packed in buyer-specified cartons with correct labelling for the destination market.

05
Step 05
Reefer Export & Delivery

Loaded into reefer containers at Chittagong Port. Full cold-chain tracking maintained throughout ocean freight to destination port worldwide.
